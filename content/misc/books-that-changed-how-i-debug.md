*side quest:* none of these are technical, and all of them made me better at the technical part.

## The list

- **Thinking in Systems** (Meadows) — once you see feedback loops, you see them in every outage.
- **The Design of Everyday Things** (Norman) — bad alerts are bad affordances; this book explains why.
- **How to Solve It** (Pólya) — a 1945 maths book that is secretly the best incident-response playbook I own.

## Why it matters

The tooling changes every year. The way you frame a problem doesn't. These sharpened the framing.
