*side quest:* it started because the office keyboard was loud. It did not stay that simple.

## The build

A 65% board, gasket-mounted, with linear switches hand-lubed one at a time while a podcast played in the background. The sound went from *clack* to *thock*, and I have opinions about that difference I will not bore you with here.

## Was it worth the money?

No. Was it worth the evenings? Surprisingly, yes — it's the most analog thing I do all week, and there are no logs to grep.
